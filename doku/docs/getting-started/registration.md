# Creating Your Scoriet Account

Getting started with Scoriet is simple. In just a few minutes, you'll have a full-featured account ready to generate your first project. Let's walk through the registration process step by step.

## What You'll Need

Before you begin, make sure you have:

- ✉️ A valid email address (you'll need this to verify your account)
- 🔐 A secure password (at least 8 characters, with letters and numbers)
- 💼 Basic information like your name and organization (optional but helpful)
- 🌐 A web browser with JavaScript enabled

:::note
Scoriet works best on modern browsers (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge). Older browser versions may have compatibility issues.
:::

## Step-by-Step Registration

### 1. Navigate to the Scoriet Sign-Up Page

Open your web browser and go to the Scoriet homepage. Look for the **"Sign Up"** or **"Create Account"** button in the top right corner.

<div class="screenshot-placeholder">Screenshot: Scoriet homepage with Sign Up button — <code>homepage.png</code></div>

### 2. Fill Out Your Information

You'll see the registration form. Here's what each field means:

| Field | Description | Required |
|-------|-------------|----------|
| **Full Name** | Your first and last name | ✅ Yes |
| **Email Address** | Your email (this becomes your login username) | ✅ Yes |
| **Password** | A secure password with mix of letters/numbers | ✅ Yes |
| **Confirm Password** | Re-enter your password to confirm | ✅ Yes |
| **Organization** | Your company or team name | ⭕ Optional |
| **Role** | Your position (Developer, Manager, Architect, etc.) | ⭕ Optional |

<div class="screenshot-placeholder">Screenshot: Registration form with all fields visible — <code>registration-form.png</code></div>

:::tip **Strong Password Tips**
- Use at least 8 characters
- Mix uppercase and lowercase letters
- Include numbers and special characters (!@#$%)
- Avoid using common words or personal information
- Don't reuse passwords from other accounts
:::

### 3. Review the Terms of Service

Before you can create your account, you'll need to agree to our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. Take a moment to review these—they outline how we protect your data and what you can do with Scoriet.

Check the box that says **"I agree to the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy"**.

<div class="screenshot-placeholder">Screenshot: Terms of Service agreement section — <code>terms-agreement.png</code></div>

:::info **Privacy First**
Scoriet takes your data security seriously. Your code and database information are encrypted and never shared with third parties. We only access what's necessary to provide the service.
:::

### 4. Click "Create Account"

Once you've filled in all required fields and agreed to the terms, click the blue **"Create Account"** button. Scoriet will process your information and send you a verification email.

<div class="screenshot-placeholder">Screenshot: Create Account button highlighted — <code>create-account-button.png</code></div>

### 5. Verify Your Email

Check your email inbox (sometimes checks your spam folder too!) for a message from **noreply@scoriet.com** with the subject **"Verify Your Scoriet Account"**.

<div class="screenshot-placeholder">Screenshot: Email verification message — <code>verification-email.png</code></div>

Click the **"Verify Email"** button or link in the email. This confirms that you own the email address and is required to activate your account.

:::caution **Email Not Received?**
- Check your spam/junk folder
- Wait a few minutes—sometimes emails take a moment to arrive
- Make sure you typed your email correctly during registration
- You can request a new verification email from the login page
:::

### 6. Email Verified! You're Ready to Go

Once you click the verification link, you'll see a success message. Your account is now active and ready to use!

<div class="screenshot-placeholder">Screenshot: Email verified success message — <code>email-verified.png</code></div>

## Account Security Best Practices

:::caution **Keep Your Password Safe**
- Never share your password with anyone
- Don't store it in plain text files or sticky notes
- Change your password regularly (every 90 days)
- Logout when using shared computers
:::

:::info **Enable Two-Factor Authentication**
Once logged in, visit your account settings to enable optional two-factor authentication (2FA). This adds an extra security layer by requiring a code from your phone during login.
:::

## Troubleshooting Registration

**"Email already registered"**  
This email is already associated with a Scoriet account. If this is your account, go to [Login](./login.md). If you forgot your password, use the password reset feature.

**"Password doesn't meet requirements"**  
Make sure your password has at least 8 characters, includes letters and numbers, and doesn't contain only sequential characters.

**"Name field is required"**  
Please enter your full name to proceed. This helps us provide better support and personalized features.

**"I can't receive the verification email"**  
Check your spam folder first. If it's still not there, you can request a new verification email from the login page—just click "Didn't receive verification email?"
